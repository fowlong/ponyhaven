PONYHAVEN 0.3.0 — WINDOWS

1. Close any older Ponyhaven copy and its game windows.
2. Right-click the ZIP and choose Extract All. Keep the whole extracted folder.
3. Open Ponyhaven-0.3.0-win32-x64 and double-click Ponyhaven.exe.
4. Sign in with your invited Firebase account. No Gumroad key is required.

Existing saves stay in your Windows profile. Keep resources and the DLLs beside
Ponyhaven.exe. No Node.js, Python, Minecraft or map conversion is needed.

Adventure opens Equestria by default; the original SRB2 courses are Bonus stages.
The converted Equestria map is included. NoCube is disabled in this revision.
Choose a pony, then New game or Load game to manage that pony's adventures.
Open party to host or join by invite code.

WASD moves; the mouse rotates the camera, including around a stationary pony.
Space jumps/uses your ability, X charges a spin dash, E interacts, I opens the
world map, and V cycles the minimap. Default draw distance is 512 blocks.
Left click sweeps a horizontal melee trail; right click fires a rainbow orb.
Orbs destroy a spherical 3-block radius without dropping collectible blocks.
Collect the seven glowing books and press 1–7 to equip or put away a book.
An equipped book replaces both mouse attacks with its primary/secondary spells.

Town ponies offer fetch quests; rings and deliveries earn bits. Fifty chests each
give one rare card or pet and refill independently after 24 hours. Sixteen giant
world bosses have telegraphed attacks and drop their own virtual-pet collectible.
Use Finish & return in Ponyhaven to exit.

Terrain damage resets each time a solo or hosted world opens; progress is kept.
Joining a live party retains its current terrain. Older progress is available
as Original adventure. New game starts a separate save; Load game also offers
save deletion. Collected cards and pets remain in your shared collection.

PS5: sticks move/look, Cross jumps, Square/Circle attack, Triangle interacts,
L1/R1 cycle books, D-pad left/right opens map/minimap, up interacts, down charges
spin dash, and Options pauses. Keyboard/mouse remain available for map menus.
Terrain has pastel blue fog and light filtering. Eight-direction pony sprites,
health bars, red enemy attack warnings and procedural music/sounds are included.

Read EQUESTRIA_0.3.0.md for controls, spell details and gameplay limits.
QA.md records automated checks and remaining manual play-feel review.
